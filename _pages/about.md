I am a graduate student at Portland State University pursuing separate M.S. degrees in Statistics and Mathematics. My academic work focuses on mathematical optimization, statistical learning, and artificial intelligence.

I have experience developing and analyzing machine learning models in Python, with particular interests in representation learning, gradient-based and proximal optimization, kernel methods, and high-dimensional statistical modeling. I am interested in using mathematical structure to develop models that are computationally efficient, interpretable, and theoretically well grounded.

I am also a Graduate Research Assistant with Portland State University's Population Research Center, where I contributed to the [Oregon Population Forecast Program's](https://www.pdx.edu/population-research/population-forecasts) published 2026 county forecast reports.
