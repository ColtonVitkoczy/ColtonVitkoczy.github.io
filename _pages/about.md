I am a graduate student at Portland State University pursuing separate M.S. degrees in Statistics and Mathematics. My work lies at the intersection of mathematical optimization, statistical learning, and artificial intelligence.

I develop and analyze machine-learning models in Python, with particular interests in representation learning, gradient-based and proximal optimization, kernel methods, and high-dimensional statistical modeling. I am interested in using mathematical structure to develop models that are computationally efficient, interpretable, and theoretically well founded.

I am also a Research Scientist with Portland State University's Population Research Center. I have contributed to the [Oregon Population Forecast Program](https://www.pdx.edu/population-research/population-forecasts) by reviewing and debugging automated R Markdown forecasting reports, validating database outputs, tables, figures, and generated narrative text, and supporting GitHub-based report development.

## Research Interests

- Mathematical optimization and convex analysis
- Statistical learning and kernel methods
- Deep learning and representation learning
- Proximal and first-order optimization algorithms
- High-dimensional statistical modeling
- Applications of machine learning to scientific and financial data

## Featured Projects

### Deep Learning for Image Reconstruction from Incomplete Data

Developed a Python machine-learning pipeline combining neural-network training with proximal-gradient optimization to reconstruct incomplete and corrupted MNIST images.

[View the project on GitHub](https://github.com/ColtonVitkoczy/sparse-image-reconstruction)

### Predictive Modeling with Kernel Ridge Regression and Nyström Approximation

Implemented Kernel Ridge Regression and low-rank Nyström approximations in Python, with simulation experiments examining prediction error, computational efficiency, stability, and approximation quality.

### Diffusion Mapping and Nonlinear Dimensionality Reduction

Compared Diffusion Maps with PCA, multidimensional scaling, and Isomap to study nonlinear structure, embedding quality, and low-dimensional representations of complex datasets.
